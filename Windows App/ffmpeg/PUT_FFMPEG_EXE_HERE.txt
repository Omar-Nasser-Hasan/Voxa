This folder is OPTIONAL. You do not need to put anything here.

The app now finds or downloads FFmpeg automatically the first time it runs (see
Services/FFmpegBootstrapper.cs and section 2 of README.md at the project root) and
caches it in the user's own profile - no manual setup needed.

Only place a ffmpeg.exe (and optionally LICENSE.txt) in this folder if you
specifically want to bundle FFmpeg for a fully offline installer, so the app never
needs to download anything even on a brand-new machine. See README.md section 2 for
exactly where to get one.

Expected file, if you choose to add it: ffmpeg.exe
